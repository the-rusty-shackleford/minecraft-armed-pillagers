About **one pillager in seven** spawns with a gun from [Ranged Weapons Mod](/wiki/ranged-weapons-mod)
instead of a crossbow, raiders included. And they know how to use it: they close to the gun's range,
keep you in sight, circle while they shoot, fire real rounds from the gun, and keep moving while they
reload.

## What they carry

On this server, 14.5% of newly spawned pillagers are armed:

| Gun | Share of armed pillagers | Of all pillagers |
|---|---|---|
| **Pistol** | 47% | about 1 in 15 |
| **Rifle** | 23% | about 1 in 29 |
| **Shotgun** | 16% | about 1 in 44 (it has to close to about 12 blocks) |
| **Scoped rifle** | 9% | about 1 in 74 (it reaches 64 blocks) |
| **Machine gun** | 5% | about 1 in 150 |

The guns hit as hard as they do in your hands: see
[the guns](/wiki/ranged-weapons-mod#the-guns). Lower damage is more common by design.

- Only pillagers spawned **after** the mod was installed can be armed. Old ones keep their crossbows.
- An armed pillager holds its gun the way it holds a crossbow.

## What they drop

When a player kills one:

| Drop | Chance |
|---|---|
| **The gun**, mostly intact (not battered like vanilla's dropped gear) | 10% |
| **Loose rounds** of its ammunition, up to 3 | 50% |
| **A partly loaded magazine**, 4 to 16 rounds | 10% |
